This We Believe

 

  • That our God is triune in personality (Father, Son and Holy Spirit), yet one God. Deuteronomy 6:4;  Matthew 3:16-17

  • That all scripture is given by the inspiration of God and is profitable for doctrine, reproof, correction and for instruction in righteousness. 2 Timothy 3:16

  • That God’s people perish from a lack of His Word.  Hosea 4:6

  • That the Word of God is alive and powerful, and that it divides asunder the soul and spirit, joints and marrow, and discerns the thoughts and intents of our hearts.  Hebrews 4:12

  • That by the hearing of God’s Word, faith comes to the believer  Romans 10:17

  • That faith is exercised by believing in the heart, confessing what one believes of the Word of God, and being
    a doer of the Word and not a hearer only.      Mark 11:23;  James 2:18

  • That through faith we have access to all the graces of God - salvation, water baptism, laying on of hands, infilling of the Holy Spirit, knowledge of His Word, answered prayer, and a personal relationship as a son of God.  Romans 5:2; Matthew 9:29

  • That God wants all believers to be healthy, prosperous and through His principle of giving and His law of sowing and reaping, we live under a more efficient economic system with God as our source of supply. 
    3 John 2;  Matthew 6:31-33

  • All believers have power over all the power of the enemy, and they are to heal the sick, cleanse the lepers, raise the dead, cast out devils and preach the good news.  Luke 10:19;  Matthew 10:7-8

  • In the manifestation of the miraculous power of God in the life of all believers, through the indwelling Holy Spirit, the fruit of the Spirit, the gifts of the Spirit and the obedience of His Word.  1 Corinthians 12:7-10; 
    Galatians 5:22

  • That Christ is an overcomer - victorious, more than a conqueror, and thus, as His Body, we are the same through Him. Ephesians 1:19-23

  • That Christ is the head of the Church and all attention and glory is directed to Him alone, and it is Christ whom we preach, praise, worship and serve.  Ephesians 1:22;  John 12:32

  • That the Head of the Church has given apostles, prophets, evangelists, pastors and teachers for the perfecting of the saints and the work of the ministry, and that these are actual, literal offices functioning in the Church today.  Ephesians 4:8-12

  • That Christ will literally come again for His Church - without spot or blemish, looking for faith in the lives of believers. Luke 18:8;  Ephesians 5:27;  1 Thessalonians 4:16-17

  • That all will be judged; the saints for their works, and the sinners, satan and all demons to the Lake of Fire.  1 Corinthians 3:13-15;  Revelation 20

  • That the saints shall reign with Him forever. 1 Thessalonians 4:17

  • That all must be “born again” to enter the Kingdom of God, and to obtain His blessings, to have peace with God, and to meet Him in the air without spot or blemish. This is done by believing in your heart and confessing with your mouth that Jesus is Lord and that God raised Him from the dead.  Romans 10:9; 
    John 3:3
