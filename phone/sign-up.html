 Once you are registered you will receive your first lesson through the mail*.

  • Your lesson will include the audio versions on MP3, the course booklet, test paper and a return envelope.

  • You will complete a study of the assigned subject and complete the course materials.

  •  When you are ready, you will then take the included exam for the topic.

  •  Finally, place your completed exam along with a free-will offering (of any size, and any currency) inside the included return envelope and  mail this to our ministry (Please keep the study and course materials, they are yours to keep).

  • We will return your graded test paper to you along with your next subject.

  • Once all of the subjects have been completed, you will receive your  diploma.

*Please allow 4-6 weeks mail delivery of course material for domestic shipping.

 

*Please allow 6-8 weeks, or even longer, mail delivery of course material on international and overseas shipping.
