Biography

Gary V. Johnson

Pastor / Founder

Gary V. Johnson has been in Christian Ministry since the early 1980's in various ministry leadership and helps positions.

 

After pioneering and pastoring a church in Canada Gary traveled to the Philippines, where he served from 1992 - 2004 as Director of Pastoral Ministries for Calvary International and as Philippine Director for The International Convention of Faith Ministries before going on to establish Gary V. Johnson Ministries, The Storehouse and Leadership Training Institute.

 

In addition to authoring numerous books and publications Gary continues to minister worldwide through seminars, conferences, the ministries Bible School Leadership Training Institute' and the ministries local church 'The Storehouse'.
